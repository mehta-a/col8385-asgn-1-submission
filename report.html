Charge and isoelectric point use the EMBOSS pKa set. GRAVY uses Kyte-Doolittle. The hydrophobic moment is the Eisenberg consensus scale over sliding windows of 11 residues at 100 degrees per residue. AUROC is rank based with tie correction. Non-standard residues contribute zero to scale-based features and are excluded from mass, so files carrying many of them will read slightly light.
